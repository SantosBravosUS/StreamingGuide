Spotify is a music streaming platform where you can listen to songs, explore albums, follow artists, create playlists, and share music. This guide explains relevant platform features and how to support SANTOS BRAVOS through genuine listening.

UNDERSTAND THE PLATFORM

HOW STREAMS ARE COUNTED

**Spotify's official rule:** A song stream is counted when someone plays the song for at least 30 seconds.
01 · PLAYBACK

30 seconds

A song must be played for at least 30 seconds to register as a stream under Spotify's counting rules.

02 · OFFLINE LISTENING

Reconnect to sync

Streams from downloaded music are counted when the listener goes online. Spotify says the app must reconnect at least once every 30 days.

03 · REPORTING

UTC time zone

Spotify for Artists uses UTC to define a reporting day. Its daily totals may therefore differ from your local calendar date.

A stream-counting threshold does not mean every music chart uses identical eligibility or ranking rules. Individual charts may have their own methodology.

MAKE IT YOURS

PLAYLISTS & LISTENING HABITS

Playlists let you collect songs around a mood, activity, artist, or personal taste. You can create your own playlist, add songs, change their order, and share it with others.

Your own playlists

Create playlists for your favorite songs, artists, albums, or listening moods.

Spotify editorial playlists

These are curated by Spotify's editorial team. Placement is not guaranteed.

Personalized playlists

Spotify also creates playlists based on individual listening habits and music preferences.

There is no official Spotify playlist sequence or fixed song interval that fans must follow. Build playlists around music you genuinely enjoy, not supposed streaming formulas.

Playlist organization example

A personal playlist might include a SANTOS BRAVOS focus track, another group song, and music from other artists. The order is your choice.

  1. SANTOS BRAVOS — Focus song
  2. Another SANTOS BRAVOS song
  3. A favorite song by another artist
  4. Another song you enjoy
  5. SANTOS BRAVOS — Another track
  6. Music from another artist

This is only an example of playlist organization, not a Spotify-approved streaming strategy.

KEEP IT AUTHENTIC

WHAT TO AVOID

Spotify prohibits artificial streaming and paid services that promise streams or playlist placement.
  • Do not use bots, scripts, or automated playback to inflate streams.
  • Do not repeatedly loop songs as an attempt to manipulate stream counts.
  • Avoid services that guarantee streams, followers, or playlist placement in exchange for money.
  • Do not coordinate tactics intended to evade Spotify's artificial-streaming detection.

Spotify says confirmed artificial streams do not count toward public stream numbers or charts and can result in additional penalties. Genuine listening is the way to support the music.
